# Sendable: instructions for raters

You will see board drafts one at a time, each with what it was for and the
source it was written from (meeting notes or an email). They are in a random
order, and nothing says where each came from.

For each, decide: **would Morgan send this draft as it is?** Morgan writes
plainly and briefly, puts the decision first in anything for the board,
expands every abbreviation, and gives a source for every figure.

| Rating | Means | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | Morgan would send it as it is. | Every fact matches the notes; the decision comes first; no shorthand. |
| 3 | Small edits only: a phrase or a line. | Right in substance, with one heading Morgan would rename. |
| 2 | Substantial edits: a wrong fact, a missing section, or the wrong register. | A date that doesn't match the notes; or chatty, with a long thank-you. |
| 1 | Morgan would rewrite it. | Misses what was asked, or reads nothing like Morgan. |

Give a one-line reason for each rating. Judge on your own: please don't look
at anyone else's ratings until you have finished.
