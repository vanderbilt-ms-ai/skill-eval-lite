# What this run was for

Wren, Morgan Reyes's assistant (fictional), was asked:

> My check-in with Dana is Wednesday at 11. Give me a short brief to read
> beforehand: what's open between us, and what I need from her. Save it in
> notes/.

The assistant's standing rules (CLAUDE.md) say: search the knowledge graph
(`kb/kb search <words>`, then `kb/kb node <title>`) before answering anything
about a person, organization, project or past decision, and report nothing
found as nothing found. The brief it wrote is output.md.
