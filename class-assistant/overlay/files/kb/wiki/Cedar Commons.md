# Cedar Commons

## Summary
A regional housing nonprofit with a staff of forty, where Morgan is Director of
Partnerships. Its funders are foundation program officers, two state agencies and a dozen
corporate partners.

## Explanation
Program delivery and the case management system belong to the program team, not to
Morgan or this assistant (Standing instructions). `Grants/current/` holds the canonical
budget workbook, the signed agreements and the pipeline sheet; superseded copies elsewhere
on the drive are not reliable (Standing instructions).
