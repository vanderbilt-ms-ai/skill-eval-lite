# Riverside Q1 finance packet

## Summary
The finance packet for Riverside's board meeting on 2026-11-19. It covers the first
quarter of Riverside's fiscal year, July to September 2026.

(Standing instructions). The board meets on 2026-11-19 (Calendars), and the packet goes to
the board a week before the meeting (Riverside packet template). Ellis Park supplies the
