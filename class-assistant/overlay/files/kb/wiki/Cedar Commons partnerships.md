# Cedar Commons partnerships

## Summary
Morgan's day job: funder relationships, grant narratives and reports, partner agreements
and MOUs, and the renewal pipeline.

## Explanation
Not program delivery and not the case management system; those belong to the program team
(Standing instructions). Downstream readers are funders and partners, so every figure is
checked against `Grants/current/` and named with its source before it goes in a draft.
