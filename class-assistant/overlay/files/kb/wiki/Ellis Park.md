# Ellis Park

## Summary
Executive director of Riverside Youth Collective and Morgan's counterpart on the
quarterly finance packet.

## Explanation
Riverside has no CFO. Ellis supplies the quarter's actuals and Morgan writes most of the
packet around them (Principal profile; Riverside packet template). Anything for the board
is drafted for Morgan to send; nothing drafted says what a board member thought or will
approve.
