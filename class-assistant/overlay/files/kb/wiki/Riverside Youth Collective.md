# Riverside Youth Collective

## Summary
A small youth organization where Morgan is a board member and chairs the finance
committee. It has no CFO, so the quarterly finance packet is mostly Morgan's writing.

## Explanation
The board meets six times a year (Principal profile). Riverside's fiscal year ends June 30,
which is offset from Cedar Commons and a common source of confusion. "The committee"
always means the finance committee unless another is named (Standing instructions). The
board are volunteers with mixed financial literacy, so every number is introduced in plain
language before it is given, and no acronym goes in unexpanded (Principal profile).
