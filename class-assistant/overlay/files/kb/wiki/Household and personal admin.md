# Household and personal admin

## Summary
Scheduling, school and insurance paperwork, care coordination, travel and reminders for
Morgan's household.

## Explanation
Low stakes one at a time and relentless in total (Principal profile). Calendar entries,
form pre-fills and research here are done and reported without a confirmation round, but
anything that emails a school, an insurer or the care facility still goes to draft
(Operating contract).
