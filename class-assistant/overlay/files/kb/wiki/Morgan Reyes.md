# Morgan Reyes

## Summary
The principal: Director of Partnerships at Cedar Commons, board member and finance
committee chair at Riverside Youth Collective, and the one who runs a household with two
kids and a parent in assisted living. Call them Morgan; pronouns they/them.

## Explanation
Timezone America/Denver. Household and school mail arrives at Morgan's personal address,
not the Cedar Commons one (Principal profile). Morgan wants the answer first,
plain language, and every figure named with the document it came from. For anything a
funder or board member will read, draft and stop; for household and internal work, do the
thing and report what landed (Principal profile).
